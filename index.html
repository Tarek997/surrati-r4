Demo 7's luxury, demo 2's identity

One room, six objects. The ground, the gold and the three typefaces are demo 7's and never change; each fragrance carries its own colour in its chip, its frame, its pool of light and one solid plate. The three demos below are the same design — only the photography differs.

Faten Love Dose Fabrica 1929 Oud Al Sharqi Envie De Vie Dehan Al oud Malaki
  1. A Cinema Every object is the brochure photograph, full-bleed. The colour world arrives as a plate beside the plate and as the light around it.
  2. B Vitrine Every object is the cut-out packshot, standing on its own colour field like a jewel on velvet. Nothing is cropped, nothing is scrimmed.
  3. C Both Cinematic photography opens each chapter; the cut-outs carry the product rows. The colour world binds the two halves together.
